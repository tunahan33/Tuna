```
Sanayideki küçük bir arsada tek bir arabayla başlıyorsun. Hedefin: şehrin en büyük otomotiv merkezinin patronu olmak!

Galeri Patronu, ikinci el araba ticaretini baştan sona yönettiğin bir işletme oyunu. İlanları kolla, satıcıyla pazarlık et, aracı atölyede hazırla, fiyat etiketini as ve müşteriyle anlaş. Her doğru hamle kasana kâr olarak döner.

AL
• Her sabah yenilenen ilanlar: sahibinden, galeriden, filo çıkışı, acil satılık ve hasarlı araçlar

SAT
• 13 farklı müşteri tipi: öğrenci, esnaf, kalabalık aile, doktor, koleksiyoncu, milyarder…
• Her müşterinin bütçesi, zevki ve pazarlık payı farklı; kimi takasa araç verir, kimi kendi ekspertizini yaptırır
• Teklifi kabul et, biraz daha iste ya da etikette diren

BÜYÜ
• 45 hayali model, 10 sınıf: ekonomiden klasiğe, spordan ultra lükse

PATRON GİBİ YAŞA
• Kazandığınla ev, araba, mücevher, takım elbise, uçak ve yat al
```

## English (oyun İngilizceye çevrildikten sonra)

**Short description — Google Play (80)**

> Buy cheap, fix up, sell for profit! Grow a dirt lot into a giant car dealership.

**Subtitle — App Store (30)**

> Buy, fix, flip, get rich!

**Keywords — App Store (100)**

> car,dealer,tycoon,simulator,business,idle,flip,auction,used cars,garage,manager

**Full description (4000)**

```
You start with one car on a small dirt lot. Your goal: become the boss of the biggest car dealership in town!

Galeri Patronu is a business game where you run a used-car dealership from start to finish. Browse listings, haggle with sellers, fix cars up in your workshop, put on a price tag and close the deal with customers.

BUY
• Fresh listings every morning: private sellers, dealers, fleet sales, urgent sales and damaged cars
• Haggle by chat – lowball too hard and the seller walks away
• Live auctions against rival dealers
• Skip the inspection and hidden defects may surface: rolled-back odometers, flood damage, undeclared paint…
• Car values are hidden at first: hire an appraiser to tell bargains from rip-offs

FIX UP
• Detailing, servicing, engine rebuilds, body and paint, dent repair, interiors and full classic restorations
• Mechanics get tired – a rest in the break hut makes them faster

SELL
• 13 customer types, each with their own budget, taste and haggling style
• Some offer a trade-in, some bring their own inspector
• Accept, ask for a little more or stand firm on your price

GROW
• 45 fictional models in 10 classes, from budget hatchbacks to hypercars
• 5 locations, 4 facilities with 20 levels each – every upgrade changes how your dealership looks
• Hire sales staff, mechanics, washers, an accountant and an appraiser

LIVE LIKE A BOSS
• Spend your profits on houses, cars, jewelry, suits, planes and yachts
• Invest in apartments, restaurants, hotels, a mall and even your own football club
• Your dealership keeps earning while you are away

FAIR ADS
• Completely free to play
• Watching ads is optional and gives you bonuses
• No ads pop up mid-game, during haggling or auctions; no banners

All car brands and models in the game are fictional.
```
